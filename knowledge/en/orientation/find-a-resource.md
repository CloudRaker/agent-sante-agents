## Need a health assessment or advice?

Official source: https://sante.quebec/en/help-and-resources/find-a-resource/

When to call Info-Santé  811 :

- **Wounds, skin conditions and skin care**

(abrasions, burns, acne, dermatitis, ulcers, etc.)

- **Infections and infectious diseases**

(influenza, gastroenteritis, COVID, STIs, urinary tract infections, etc.)

- **Cardiovascular and neurological health**

(stroke, hypertension, heart failure, circulatory disorders, etc.)

- **Respiratory health**

(asthma, bronchitis, COPD, pneumonia, etc.)

- **Digestive health and nutrition**

(constipation, diarrhea, reflux, dehydration, nutrition, etc.)

- **Pain and general symptoms**

(fever, pain, dizziness, fatigue, headache, etc.)

- **Women’s, men’s and perinatal health**

(pregnancy, breastfeeding, menopause, prostate health, etc.)

- **Child and adolescent health**

(fever in children, ear infections, childhood rashes, etc.)

- **Lifestyle habits and prevention**

(smoking, physical activity, vaccination, fall prevention, etc.)

Psychosocial and mental health counselling

When to call Info-Social 811

- Anxiety
- Violence
- Grief
- Mental health
- Youth in difficulty
- Sexuality
- Family or relationship problems
- Addiction
- Relationship breakup
- Suicidal thoughts
- Any concerning behaviour

If you do not have a family doctor, this option will refer you to the appropriate health resources based on your clinical or psychosocial needs.

Free, confidential service, available 24 hours a day, 365 days a year.

**Online option**

- Assess the user’s health needs (appointments, home care, psychosocial support, etc.) using a questionnaire.
- A healthcare professional follows up by phone if needed to carry out a more in-depth assessment and direct the person to the most appropriate resources (pharmacy, clinic, blood tests, etc.).

For assistance completing the digital form, call 811 and select Option 3.

[Online GAP](https://www.quebec.ca/sante/trouver-une-ressource/guichet-acces-premiere-ligne)

- If you have been assigned an FMG but not a family doctor or specialized nurse practitioner (SNP).

**Online**

Fill out the form at the Primary Care Access Point.

- If you have been assigned a family doctor or specialized nurse practitioner (SNP), but they are unavailable for a consultation in the coming days or weeks,

**By phone**

- contact your FMG to make an appointment with someone else.

Services offered:

- Assessment of a person’s physical and mental condition to ensure the appropriate use of medications
- Renewal of a prescription, which cannot exceed the validity period of the original prescription or one year
- Prescription of laboratory tests to monitor drug therapy
- Prescription of medications, particularly to treat minor health problems or when no diagnosis is required
- Independent adjustment of drug therapy parameters, such as dosage or dosing schedule, to ensure patient safety
- Substitution of a medication in the event of a supply shortage, if there is an issue related to its administration or if the medication poses a safety risk
- Management of certain chronic diseases (e.g., diabetes, hypertension, hypothyroidism)
- Administration of a medication in an emergency situation or to demonstrate proper use
- Prescription and administration of vaccines

To understand your symptoms, learn what you can do at home, and know when to seek medical care.

## Need an appointment today?

Official source: https://sante.quebec/en/help-and-resources/find-a-resource/

**Online**

Fill out the form at the Primary Care Access Point.

**By phone**

- contact your FMG to make an appointment with someone else.

Online option

The service allows you to:

- Schedule an appointment with a healthcare professional (family doctor, specialized nurse practitioner or resident physician) working in a clinic near a location of your choice.
- Cancel an appointment booked online.
- Receive confirmation of your appointment and reminders by email, automated phone call and text message (SMS).

Online, free and secure.
