# Local point of service

Official source: https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/health-system-and-services/service-organization/local-point-service

> The systematic offer of rapid tests for COVID-19 screening has been discontinued. Rapid tests may no longer be available at certain local points of service.
>
> [People eligible](https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/advice-and-prevention/screening-and-carrier-testing-offer/testing-flu-like-symptoms/get-covid-19-test#c148948) for local points of service testing for influenza-like symptoms or COVID-19 can make an [appointment](https://portal3.clicsante.ca/services/depistage-de-la-covid-19).

Local points of service offer vaccination, sample collection, and screening services to Quebecers. They help improve access to preventive health services, including by making it easier to book appointments.

## Services offered

Official source: https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/health-system-and-services/service-organization/local-point-service

The services offered differ by service location and the needs of each region. They include:

- COVID-19, influenza, and shingles vaccines
- COVID-19 and influenza screening
- Group A streptococcus screening
- Colorectal cancer screening
- Blood, stool and urine sample collection
- Vaccination under the [Québec Immunization Program](https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/advice-and-prevention/vaccination/quebec-immunisation-program)

## Make an appointment

Official source: https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/health-system-and-services/service-organization/local-point-service

To make an appointment at your local point of service, visit [www.clicsante.ca](https://portal3.clicsante.ca/).

You can also call [1-877-644-4545](tel:18776444545) for information or to book an appointment for your whooping cough vaccination:

- Monday to Friday, 8 a.m. to 6 p.m.
    - A time slot from 6 p.m. to 8 p.m. is also set aside for vaccination and screening appointments.
- Saturdays and Sundays from 8 a.m. to 6 p.m. (appointments for vaccination and screening only).
- Calls on weekdays after 6 p.m. and on weekends are handled by agents from the regional call centers of the health and social services network.
