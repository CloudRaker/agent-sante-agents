# Situation in emergency rooms in Québec

Official source: https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/health-system-and-services/service-organization/quebec-health-system-and-its-services/situation-in-emergency-rooms-in-quebec

> Please note that data regarding emergency rooms in the regions of Mauricie–Centre-du-Québec and Nord-de-l’Île-de-Montréal are unavailable. We are working to resolve the issue as quickly as possible.

Check the situation of the emergency rooms closest to you and for all of Québec.

Note that the estimated waiting time to see a doctor 1 depends on the number of nurses and doctors on site and the health condition of the patients in the emergency department at the time of your arrival. [Urgent cases](https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/health-system-and-services/service-organization/primary-care-health-and-social-services/when-go-emergency-room) are treated in order of priority.

> ## Is the emergency room the best option for you?
>
> Explore [options other than emergency](https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/finding-a-resource) if your condition does not require immediate assistance.

Some emergency services are not open 24 hours a day or may be subject to planned reorganization. Before you go, check the facility opening hours.
