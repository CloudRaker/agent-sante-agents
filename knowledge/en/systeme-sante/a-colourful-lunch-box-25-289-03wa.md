# A colourful lunch box

A colourful<br>

**A healthy diet can help give your child more energy, focus better and grow up healthy.**

**Here are some tips to make it easier to prepare nutritious, varied and tasty lunches!**

## Plan the menu

Official source: https://publications.msss.gouv.qc.ca/msss/fichiers/2025/25-289-03WA.pdf

Have everyone in the family participate in making a list of your favourite meals and snacks.

Look at flyers to find discounted foods. To save on the cost of groceries, cook as much as possible.

Double your recipes so you have extra for lunches or freeze them.

Prepare what you are going to put in the lunch box

You can use the Canada’s Food Guide (CFG) balanced plate as a guide. The image shows the three nutritious food groups. It also shows the recommended proportions so that the lunch box will be balanced. This includes snacks.

To save time, reusable containers of nutritious foods can be prepared ahead of time: vegetables, fruits, roasted chickpeas, pieces of cheese, plain fortified soy beverage, etc.

Prepare the lunch box with your child

This way, they:

will enjoy their lunch even more; can guide you on the amount of food; will develop their food skills;

will be proud they helped.

## Encourage your child to listen to their hunger

Official source: https://publications.msss.gouv.qc.ca/msss/fichiers/2025/25-289-03WA.pdf

Your child’s hunger may vary from day to day. So it’s up to them to decide how much of the food in their lunch box they eat.
