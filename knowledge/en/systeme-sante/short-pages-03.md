## How to get advice about a non-urgent health problem (https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/health-system-and-services/service-organization/primary-care-health-and-social-services/how-get-advice-about-non-urgent-health-problem-1)

Official source: https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/health-system-and-services/service-organization/primary-care-health-and-social-services/how-get-advice-about-non-urgent-health-problem-1

### Contact Info-Santé by calling 811

For advice regarding a non-urgent health problem, you can contact Info-Santé by calling 811 (option 1). A nurse will answer your questions, make recommendations and, if necessary, refer you to an appropriate resource in your area. The service is available 24 hours a day, 365 days a year.

You can call Info-Santé when, for example:

- You want professional advice on whether it would be best to see your doctor or another health professional or to go to emergency, because, for example, you have had the flu for several days or your child woke up with a high fever
- You want advice about treating a minor health problem

## About Québec Health Record (https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/your-health-information/quebec-health-record/about)

Official source: https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/your-health-information/quebec-health-record/about

The Québec Health Record (QHR) is a communication platform that facilitates the secure and timely sharing of health information between authorized organizations and stakeholders. It supports their delivery of health services for the benefit of users.

Authorized providers may access and consult the information in your QHR. Therefore, the QHR cannot be accessed by all health and social services providers.

### Entry of health information

When you consult a health and social services professional (family medicine group, CLSC, emergency room, etc.), your health information is entered in the local record kept at each location where you consult. Some, not all, of the information entered in the local record is automatically shared and stored in the QHR. Only professionals at the location where you consulted can access the information in your local record.

You do not have to register with the QHR. Authorized health information is automatically and systematically collected in the QHR.

### Objectives of the Québec Health Record

The objective of the QHR is to improve the quality of care and efficiency of Québec's health care system.

Thanks to the QHR, doctors and other authorized health and social services professionals in Québec who are responsible for your care can provide you with better care and more efficient follow-up. For instance, the QHR allows:

- professionals to access certain health information directly, no matter what region in Québec you consult in;
- medical attention to be provided more quickly, especially in an emergency situation, since it takes less time to find certain health information;
- better management of your samples and examinations and even means that you will not have to have the same sample collected or the examination done several times if you have to see more than one health and social services professional;
- optimal adjustment of your medication by sharing your medication history and results which, in particular, helps prevent adverse drug interactions;
- better interprofessional collaboration, since information can be obtained about the health and social services professionals who are responsible for your care and the facilities where you consult.

## Bodies authorized - Québec Health Record (https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/your-health-information/quebec-health-record/authorized-bodies)

Official source: https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/your-health-information/quebec-health-record/authorized-bodies

Some bodies are authorized to access your health information through the Québec Health Record.

The [Regulation respecting access authorizations and the duration of use of information held in a health information bank in a clinical domain](https://www.legisquebec.gouv.qc.ca/fr/document/rc/P-9.0001,%20r.%201?langCont=en) specifies the information each type of body can release or consult. At least one authorized provider must perform their duties in the body in order for access authorizations to be granted to that body.

### QHR access for healthcare bodies

**Health and social services institutions** may:

- View medication information
- Share medication information, if the institution employs at least one pharmacist
- View laboratory results
- Transmit laboratory results, if the institution operates a medical biology laboratory or if it requests lab tests from Héma-Québec, Centre de toxicologie du Québec, or Laboratoire de santé publique du Québec
- View medical imaging results
- Transmit medical imaging results, if the institution has a clinical medical imaging department

**Community pharmacies** may:

- View medication information
- Transmit medication information
- View laboratory results
- View medical imaging results

**Medical biology laboratories** may transmit laboratory results.

**General medical imaging laboratories and medical diagnostic radiology laboratories** may transmit medical imaging results.

**Private doctor’s offices, private professional’s offices, and specialized medical centres** may:

- View medication information
- View laboratory results
- View medical imaging results
- View hospitalization summaries

**Indigenous health and social services centres** or **Indigenous residential long-term care or addiction treatment centres** may:

- View medication information
- View laboratory test results
- View medical imaging results

## Available information - Québec Health record (https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/your-health-information/quebec-health-record/available-information)

Official source: https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/your-health-information/quebec-health-record/available-information

### Health information

Only the information provided for by the [_Act respecting the sharing of certain health information_](https://www.legisquebec.gouv.qc.ca/fr/document/lc/P-9.0001?langCont=en)can be recorded by the Québec Health Record (QHR).

It is organized into six clinical domains:

- **Medication domain**

    - Available information:
        - Medications prescribed or sold under pharmaceutical control and dispensed in community pharmacies.
        - Medications administered in outpatient oncology clinics:
            - Medications recognized by Health Canada,
            - Medications in Health Canada's Special Access Program,
            - Research protocol medications.

    Data on medications administered in outpatient oncology clinics in all hospital centres will soon be available in the QHR. At this time, this data is only available to certain users.

- **Laboratory domain**
    - Available information:
        - The results of laboratory tests done in an institution or a private medical biology laboratory in Québec.
- **Medical imaging**
    - Available information:
        - The results of medical imaging examinations done in an institution or a private general medical imaging or medical diagnostic radiology laboratory.
- **Immunization domain**
    - Forthcoming
        - Vaccines administered in Québec.
- **Allergy and intolerancedomain**
    - Forthcoming:
        - Allergies and intolerances.

To find out how long a body has been communicating health information to the QHR, see the [list of medical imaging sites connected to the Québec Health Record](https://publications.msss.gouv.qc.ca/msss/document-002117/) (in French only).

### System and registers

In addition to the six clinical domains, the Québec Health Record (QHR) also includes a system and several registers that collect other information.

- An electronic prescription management system for medication that allows the health professional you consult to send prescriptions to the pharmacist who will fill the prescription;
- A register of users, where information about each user’s identity is kept;
- A register of providers, where information about health and social services professionals is kept;
- A register of bodies, where information about bodies and facilities that provide health and social services is kept;
- A register of refusals that is used to manage refusals to allow information to be released through the DSQ.

## Framework for the use of health information in the Québec Health Record (https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/your-health-information/quebec-health-record/framework-for-the-use-of-health-information)

Official source: https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/your-health-information/quebec-health-record/framework-for-the-use-of-health-information

### Use of information in the Québec Health Record

The health information in the Québec Health Record (QHR) can be released on request to authorized providers and bodies in accordance with their access authorization. Under the law and regulations, it remains available for a maximum of seven years.

### Use of information obtained from the Québec Health Record and entered in the local record

Information obtained from the QHR and entered in the local record may not be released to anyone else without your **written consent**. However, it may be released, without your consent, to prevent an act of violence or where there is a serious risk of death or severe injury giving rise to an emergency situation.

When an authorized provider provides health or social services, they can add information obtained through the QHR to your local medical record, for example, your record in a family medicine group (FMG). This addition is then protected by the rules of confidentiality that apply to the local record.

### Exceptions

Even if you have given your consent, your health information may not be transmitted, in whole or in part, to:

- A person or a partnership who does verifications or acts as an expert, for example, a physician with the Société de l’assurance automobile du Québec (SAAQ) or the Commission des normes, de l’équité, de la santé et de la sécurité du travail (CNESST)
- Anyone for the purposes of a contract that requires an assessment of your health, for example an insurance contract or an employment contract

## Health information security in the Québec Health Record (https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/your-health-information/quebec-health-record/health-information-security)

Official source: https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/your-health-information/quebec-health-record/health-information-security

A number of measures and mechanisms are in place to ensure the safety, confidentiality, availability, integrity, accessibility and irrevocability of the health information in the QHR.

### How it works

Information about your identity is not kept in the same information banks as those that contain your health information. It is only when a provider accesses the QHR that all the information he is authorized to access is brought together and appears on his screen.

### System reliability

The systems used for the QHR comply with cybersecurity standards established and tested by internationally recognized standardization bodies.

As for all large-scale information technology systems, there are backup systems should a technical failure occur. These measures are intended to protect the information and ensure access to the QHR at all times.

Last update: May 6, 2021
