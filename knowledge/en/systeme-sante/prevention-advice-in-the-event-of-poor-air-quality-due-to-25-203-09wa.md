# Prevention advice in the event of poor air quality due to vegetation fires

Official source: https://publications.msss.gouv.qc.ca/msss/fichiers/2025/25-203-09WA.pdf

**IN THE EVENT OF POOR AIR QUALITY DUE TO VEGETATION FIRES**

**WHEN AN AIR QUALITY WARNING IS ISSUED FOR YOUR TERRITORY :**

**Check PM2,5 concentration in real time on the** **AQMap.**

Select the sensor closest to your location and adapt your activities by referring to this table.

|**PM2,5**<br>**(µg/m****3****)**|**AIR QUALITY**|**OUTDOOR ACTIVITY ADVICE**|**INDOOR ENVIRONMENT ADVICE**|
|---|---|---|---|

|30-60|Poor for people<br>at risk|Consider reducing or rescheduling strenuous activities<br>outdoors for people at risk or those experiencing<br>symptoms.|Close windows and exterior doors. ## PREVENTION ADVICE

Official source: https://publications.msss.gouv.qc.ca/msss/fichiers/2025/25-203-09WA.pdf

**IN THE EVENT OF POOR AIR QUALITY DUE TO VEGETATION FIRES**

**PAY ATTENTION TO PEOPLE WHO MAY BE MORE AT RISK FROM SMOKE  :**

Babies and young People with children (0-4 years) respiratory, heart disease, or diabetes

Pregnant Seniors women (65 and over)

**WATCH FOR SIGNS TAKE ACTION AND SYMPTOMS IN CASE OF SYMPTOMS MILD** Go inside Keep asthma Smarting eyes and stay there. medications handy. Runny nose Mild cough Sore throat Headache Call **Info-santé 811** Tell loved ones **MORE SEVERE** or **911** depending as needed Severe cough on the severity of symptoms. Shortness of breath Wheezing

- Dizziness

- Heart palpitations

- Chest pain

**FOR MORE INFORMATION, CONSULT: QUÉBEC.CA/EFFECTS-WILDFIRE-SMOKE**
