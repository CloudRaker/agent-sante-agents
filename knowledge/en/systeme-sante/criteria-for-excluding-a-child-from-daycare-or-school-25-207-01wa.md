# Criteria for excluding a child from daycare or school

Official source: https://publications.msss.gouv.qc.ca/msss/fichiers/2025/25-207-01WA.pdf

Criteria for Excluding a Child from Childcare Setting or School Environment

Has the child had a **fever** * in the morning at home? **Yes No** Has the child developed a **fever** * at school? OR Has a child under 3 months of age developed a **fever** * while in daycare? OR **~~Yes~~** If a child aged 3 months or older has developed a **fever** * while in daycare, give them, if indicated, acetaminophen according to protocol. **Is the fever* still present 1 hour later? No** Does the child have a **health condition** that prevents them from participating in group activities? Does their health condition prevent **~~Yes~~** the person in charge from adequately caring for the other children? **No** Has the child **vomited two or more times** in the last 24 hours? **~~Yes~~ No** Has the child had episodes of **diarrhea** that were too heavy to be contained in a diaper OR contained blood or mucus? **~~Yes~~** OR Is the child incontinent (unable to get to the bathroom)? **No** Does the child have **skin lesions with purulent discharge (pus)** **~~Yes~~** that cannot be covered with a waterproof bandage? **No** Has the child been instructed by a healthcare professional **Yes** to be temporarily excluded from the environment?

**No** Has the child been instructed by a healthcare professional to be temporarily excluded from the environment? **No There is no need to exclude the child.** Monitor their health and reinforce basic practices such as hand hygiene, respiratory hygiene and etiquette, and cleaning and disinfecting objects, surfaces, and rooms.

_This tool should never replace a consultation with a healthcare professional or the instructions associated with the diagnosis._

Exclude the child from the environment.<br>He or she must stay at home.<br>

## A return will be permitted when all of the following conditions apply:

Official source: https://publications.msss.gouv.qc.ca/msss/fichiers/2025/25-207-01WA.pdf

- **No fever*.**

- **The child’s general condition** allows them to participate in group activities and requires little or no additional care.

- **No vomiting** in the last 24 hours.

- **Diarrhea:** Stools are contained in the diaper, or the child is continent. No blood or mucus in the diarrhea.

- **If purulent skin lesions are present:** No discharge, or possibility of covering lesions with a waterproof bandage.

- **If excluded due to instructions from a healthcare professional:** Return to the setting according to instructions

*** Definition of fever:** Child under 3 months old:

- 38.0 °C or higher (rectum)

- Child 3 months or older:

- 38.5 °C or above (rectum)

- 38.0 °C or above (mouth, but only applies to children aged 5 years and older)

- 37.5 °C or above (armpit)

**Rectal temperature is more reliable.**
