# Financial assistance for transportation to an institution in the health and social services network

Official source: https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/health-system-and-services/medical-transportation/financial-assistance-medical-transportation

The cost of transportation to an institution in the health and social services network is the responsibility of the person being transported, unless he or she is eligible for a government financial assistance program.

Financial assistance is available for people who:

- need to move within the same institution
- must transfer to another institution
- are over 65 years of age
- are receiving end-of-life care
- are geographically distant by more than 200 km from the institution where they are transported
- are receiving oncological treatments
- are waiting for a transplant or undergoing post-transplant follow-up
- are from an indigenous community

These people can receive financial assistance when they need to be transported to or within:

- a local community services centre
- a hospital centre
- a residential and long-term care centre
- a child and youth protection centre
- a rehabilitation centre

The amount and terms of financial assistance depend on the local policies of Québec's regions. Each territorial establishment has determinated its own policy on user travel.

To find out which establishment you belong to, consult the page [Find Your Health and Social Services Facility](https://www.sante.gouv.qc.ca/en/repertoire-ressources/votre-cisss-ciusss/).
