## Compensation (https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/blood-tissue-and-organ-donation/compensation-plan-for-victims-of-a-hema-quebec-product/compensation)

Official source: https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/blood-tissue-and-organ-donation/compensation-plan-for-victims-of-a-hema-quebec-product/compensation

The plan mainly covers the following types of compensation:

- Income replacement compensation
- Bodily injury compensation
- Personal assistance compensation
- Death and funeral costs compensation
- Reimbursement of expenses incurred due to bodily injury, including medical expenses
- Rehabilitation compensation

In the event of a death, the victim’s succession is entitled to compensation starting from the date of death.

The compensation payable under the plan is substituted to all the rights and recourses against Héma-Québec, its administrators and employees due to the bodily injuries suffered.

Under this plan, compensation is granted regardless of the responsibility or possible errors by the institutions or parties involved (e.g., health professionals, manufacturers, distributors, quality control inspectors).

Last update: May 6, 2019

## Description (https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/blood-tissue-and-organ-donation/compensation-plan-for-victims-of-a-hema-quebec-product/description)

Official source: https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/blood-tissue-and-organ-donation/compensation-plan-for-victims-of-a-hema-quebec-product/description

The compensation plan for victims of a Héma-Québec product provides compensation to any individual whose health has been seriously affected by a defective or contaminated product distributed by Héma-Québec, despite all safety measures established.

Among other things, Héma-Québec distributes blood products, human tissue, stem cells and breast milk.

### Exceptions

All products distributed by Héma-Québec are covered by the plan, with the exception of the following:

- Products used for research purposes or for clinical trials, unless notice to the contrary is given by the Minister of Health and Social Services.
- Products developed from a human biological product excluded from the compensation plan by the Government of Québec. This exclusion also applies to products that could be added to Héma-Québec’s mandate and could potentially be manufactured from excluded human biological products.

Last update: July 6, 2022
