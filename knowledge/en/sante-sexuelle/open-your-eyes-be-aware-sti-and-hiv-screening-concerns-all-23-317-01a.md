**Sexually transmitted and bloodborne infections (STBBIs) may not have any visible signs or symptoms, especially in people who have been recently infected. People who are infected can spread the infection without knowing it.**

#### WHAT ARE STBBIs?
**THEY ARE:**

- **Chlamydia, gonorrhoea, syphilis, HIV infection, hepatitis B and C, as well as, lymphogranuloma venereum, genital herpes and condylomas.**

###### THEY CAN SPREAD:
- **through unprotected oral, vaginal or anal sex;**

- **through direct or indirect contact with the blood of someone who is infected;**

- **by sharing steroid or drug injection or inhalation equipment;**

- **during pregnancy or delivery.**

**These infections and the complications that follow are avoidable, and most of them can be treated.**

**REASONS FOR ASKING FOR AN STBBI SCREENING TEST**

###### STBBIs AFFECT MANY PEOPLE
**A****-You or your partner have had sex with:**

- **a new partner;**

- **someone who has had several partners;**

- **a partner who is from a country where STBBIs are very common;**

- **someone who is or has previously been incarcerated;**

- **a man who has had sex with men;**

- **someone who has an HIV infection;**

- **someone who currently has an STBBI or who has had one in the past;**

- **a sex worker;**
