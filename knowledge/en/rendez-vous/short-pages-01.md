## Schedule an appointment online with a family doctor (https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/finding-a-resource/family-doctor-schedule-an-appointment)

Official source: https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/finding-a-resource/family-doctor-schedule-an-appointment

Québec Medical Appointment Scheduler is a free service that lets you schedule an appointment online for a medical consultation. It is available to anyone who has a card issued by the Régie de l'assurance maladie du Québec (RAMQ), even if the card has expired or is invalid.

[Go to Québec Medical Appointment Scheduler](https://www.rvsq.gouv.qc.ca/en/public/Pages/home.aspx)

> Service in English is reserved for individuals covered by the exceptions stipulated in the _Charter of the French language_. If you have navigated to this content, you confirm in good faith that you are such an individual.

This service is secure. It lets you:

- Schedule an appointment with a health professional (family doctor, specialized nurse practitioner or medical resident) practicing at a clinic near a location of your choice
- Cancel an appointment taken online
- Receive confirmation of your appointment and reminders by email, automated phone call or text message

### Eligibility

You must have a health insurance card to access Québec Medical Appointment Scheduler.

If your health insurance card has expired or is no longer valid, you can still use the service and make an appointment. **You will have to sort out your situation with the Régie de l'assurance maladie du Québec before you go to your appointment.**

The Québec Medical Appointment Scheduler service is not available for travellers, international students, etc. who do not have a RAMQ card.

### Canceling an appointment

If your appointment with a health professional is no longer necessary, it is important to cancel it. Québec Medical Appointment Scheduler allows you to cancel an appointment scheduled on this platform or provided by the Primary Care Access Point (Guichet d’accès à la première ligne – GAP).

If you scheduled your appointment directly with your clinic, please contact the clinic to cancel.

If the appointment was scheduled on another platform, use the same platform to cancel it.

## How to get advice for children aged from 0 to 17 years (https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/health-system-and-services/service-organization/primary-care-health-and-social-services/how-get-advice-medical-appointment-children-aged-0-17-years)

Official source: https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/health-system-and-services/service-organization/primary-care-health-and-social-services/how-get-advice-medical-appointment-children-aged-0-17-years

### Contact the Info-Santé pediatric line by calling 811 (option 1)

To get advice about a health problem for a child afed from of 0 to 17 years, you can reach the Info-Santé pediatric line by calling 811 (option 1) (then option 1 for the pediatric line).

A phone line reserved for the parents of children aged from 0 to 17 years is available. You can quickly talk with an Info-Santé nurse to have your child assessed and to get advice on how to care for your child at home.

If Info-Santé confirms that your child needs medical attention, you will be transferred to a telephone reception service that will guide you toward the right resources.

## Help (https://www.rvsq.gouv.qc.ca/info/add-3-minutes.html)

Official source: https://www.rvsq.gouv.qc.ca/info/add-3-minutes.html

When you select the date and time of your appointment, Québec Medical Appointment Scheduler reserves this time-slot for 3 minutes. During this time, you can provide your contact information and confirm the appointment. If you have not finished by 2 minutes 30 seconds, the following message will appear:

By answering “Yes,” you will have an additional 3 minutes to enter your information. You can add a further 3 minutes as many times as you wish. If you answer “No,” you will have to enter your information in the time remaining.

You have 30 seconds to answer the question. If the 30 seconds are up, the appointment may be reserved by someone else.

When the 30 seconds are up, if no one else has scheduled this appointment, you will be able to continue to reserve it; otherwise, you will have to restart an appointment search.

## Help (https://www.rvsq.gouv.qc.ca/info/cancelling-appointment.html)

Official source: https://www.rvsq.gouv.qc.ca/info/cancelling-appointment.html

You can cancel an appointment in 3 ways, using the:

- Reference number
- Button appearing in the email
- Link found in the text message (SMS)

The clinic is responsible for establishing the deadline given to patients for canceling appointments **online**. The deadline, varying according to clinic, is **24 h or less before the appointment**. Past this deadline, patients are invited to call the clinic directly to cancel an appointment. In such case, the clinic may charge a cancelation fee.

If you have more than one appointment to cancel, you must cancel them one at a time.

**If you have several appointments, pay special attention to the date and time of the appointment you wish to cancel. Cancelation of an appointment is irreversible.**

#### Reference number

1. Access Québec Medical Appointment Scheduler
2. Click on the "Look up an appointment" button
3. Enter your personal information and click on “Continue”
4. Click on “Update my contact information or cancel my appointment”
5. Enter the reference number and click on “Search”

6. Click on “Cancel my appointment”
7. Click on “Yes, cancel my appointment”

#### Button appearing in the email

1. Click on the link
2. Enter your personal information and click on “Continue”
3. Click on “Cancel my appointment”
4. Click on “Yes, cancel my appointment”

#### Link found in the text message (SMS)

1. Click on the link
2. Enter your personal information and click on “Continue
3. Click on “Cancel my appointment”
4. Click on “Yes, cancel my appointment”

## Help (https://www.rvsq.gouv.qc.ca/info/means-of-communication.html)

Official source: https://www.rvsq.gouv.qc.ca/info/means-of-communication.html

Different means of communication enable you to receive appointment confirmations and reminders, as well as messages of general interest, automatically by email, automated telephone call or text message (SMS), according to your preference. Email is the default means, but you can unmark this option when you schedule an appointment or, later, by [updating your contact information](https://www.rvsq.gouv.qc.ca/info/updating-contact-info.html).

### Types of confirmation and reminder

**Cancellation:** Confirms the cancellation of the appointment.

**Confirmation:** Confirms the appointment after it has been reserved. For text messaging (SMS), a second text message is transmitted to notify you that a fee may apply depending on your service package with your cell phone company.

**Contact information changed:** Confirms that your contact information was changed with regard to an appointment.

**Change of appointment by the clinic:** Confirms that the clinic has changed the appointment. For any question on the change, contact the clinic directly.

**Reminder:** A reminder will be sent on the day before the appointment. In certain cases, based on the parameters selected by the clinic, a reminder may also be sent 3 days before. Reminders are sent between 8:00 a.m. and 9:00 p.m., 7 days a week.

**Notice:** Notifies you that you have missed a medical appointment.

## Help (https://www.rvsq.gouv.qc.ca/info/sheduling-appointment-by-proximity.html)

Official source: https://www.rvsq.gouv.qc.ca/info/sheduling-appointment-by-proximity.html

### You don’t have a family doctor

You must select “Schedule an appointment in a clinic close to me.” The search criteria are the date, reason for the consultation, postal code and search perimeter. The distance in kilometres is as the crow flies, i.e. not driving distance. As a result, the distance to get to the clinic could be greater than the distance indicated.

If you are not satisfied with any of the results, refer to the [Répertoire des ressources en santé et services sociaux](https://sante.gouv.qc.ca/en/repertoire-ressources/). The link is on the bottom of the calendar displaying appointments close to you. Please note that you cannot schedule an appointment online with this option.

### You have a family doctor

Québec Medical Appointment Scheduler first displays your assigned health professional’s available time slots. Check whether the time slots offered are suitable for you.

If none of the appointment times offered is convenient, you can contact your clinic to schedule an appointment or click on “Schedule an appointment with another health professional of my family medicine group (GMF)” if your clinic is part of such a group. Another health professional of the group may be available at your usual clinic or at another clinic.

If the appointment times offered are still not convenient, you can contact your clinic to schedule an appointment or click on “Schedule an appointment in a clinic close to me.”

The search criteria are the date, reason for the consultation, postal code and search perimeter. The distance in kilometres is as the crow flies, i.e. not driving distance. As a result, the distance to get to the clinic could be greater than the distance indicated.

If you are not satisfied with any of the results, you can contact your clinic directly. You can also refer to the [Répertoire des ressources en santé et services sociaux](https://sante.gouv.qc.ca/en/repertoire-ressources/) . The link is on the bottom of the calendar displaying appointments close to you. Please note that you cannot schedule an appointment online with this option.

## Help (https://www.rvsq.gouv.qc.ca/info/updating-contact-info.html)

Official source: https://www.rvsq.gouv.qc.ca/info/updating-contact-info.html

You must update your contact information for each upcoming appointment. This will allow the clinic to get in touch with you, as needed, and you will be able to receive appointment confirmations and reminders. Please note that updating your contact information does not transfer to your file at the la Régie de l’assurance maladie du Québec; it simply applies to the information concerning a given appointment.

The changeable information is the following:

- Email
- Home phone number
- Mobile phone number
- Means of communication (email, telephone or SMS)
- Language of communication (French or English)

You have to enter a phone number.

You can update your contact information in 3 ways, using the:

- Reference number
- Button appearing in the email
- Link found in the text message (SMS)
