# Request for Access to or Modification of Vaccination Records

Official source: https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/your-health-information/quebec-vaccination-registry/procedure-for-requesting-personal-information

Anyone may request a copy of their personal information contained in the Québec Vaccination Registry.

It is also possible to:

- view your vaccination record;
- request an update or correction if any information is inaccurate or incomplete

Requests for access to vaccination information cannot be submitted by telephone.

## Request access to your vaccination records

Official source: https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/your-health-information/quebec-vaccination-registry/procedure-for-requesting-personal-information

#### Online

This is the fastest way to obtain access to a vaccination record. It is available in French and English for individuals 14 years of age or older.

1. Go to the [Votre Santé](https://votre.sante.quebec/en-CA/) portal.
2. At the bottom of the page, in the “View your health information” section, sign in using a Government Authentication Service account.
3. Click “Vaccination”.
4. Click the download icon to save a copy of the record.

#### In person

A paper copy of your vaccination record can be obtained from a health and social services centre.

This option also makes it possible to submit a request on behalf of a person under 14 years of age.

1. Contact the [Health and Social Services Facility](https://sante.gouv.qc.ca/en/repertoire-ressources/votre-cisss-ciusss/) in your region to find out the procedure to follow.
2. Bring a valid form of identification, such as:
    - a health insurance card;
    - a birth certificate.

For a person under 14 years of age, a valid form of identification for the parent or guardian must also be provided.

#### By mail

To receive a copy of your vaccination record by mail, allow approximately 20 calendar days for processing. Processing times may vary depending on the volume of requests received. No acknowledgment of receipt is sent when a request is received.

1. Complete and sign the [Access to information on vaccines request form](https://publications.msss.gouv.qc.ca/msss/en/document-001089/).
    If you do not have a health insurance number issued by the _Régie de l’assurance maladie du Québec_ (RAMQ), it is not necessary to provide one.
2. Include a photocopy of a valid form of identification, such as:
    - a health insurance card;
    - a birth certificate.
3. Submit the request.

Consult the list of [officers in charge of requests for access to information on vaccines](https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/your-health-information/quebec-vaccination-registry/officers-in-charge-of-requests-for-access-to-information-on-vaccines) to find the mailing address for the officer responsible for your region.

## Request for a correction

Official source: https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/your-health-information/quebec-vaccination-registry/procedure-for-requesting-personal-information

Some information in your vaccination record may be incomplete or inaccurate.

To request an update or correction, contact the [health and social services facility](https://sante.gouv.qc.ca/en/repertoire-ressources/votre-cisss-ciusss/) in your region to learn about the procedure to follow.
