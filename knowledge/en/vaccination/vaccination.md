## General information

Official source: https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/advice-and-prevention/vaccination

Getting vaccinated is the best way to protect yourself against some serious illnesses. Discover all the information and advice to help you make an informed decision for you, your children and your relatives.

Vaccination is the most effective way to prevent the spread of serious infectious diseases. To understand how vaccines work, to separate fact from fiction regarding information about vaccination and get some advice, consult the following pages:

- [Understanding vaccines](https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/advice-and-prevention/vaccination/understanding-vaccines)
- [Recommended immunization schedule](https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/advice-and-prevention/vaccination/quebec-immunisation-program#c25403)
- [Procedure for getting vaccinated](https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/advice-and-prevention/vaccination/quebec-immunisation-program#c2568)
- Information regarding [Vaccination for children](https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/advice-and-prevention/vaccination/vaccination-for-children)
- Advices for [Reducing the pain and anxiety of vaccination in children](https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/advice-and-prevention/vaccination/reducing-pain-and-anxiety-of-vaccination-children)
- [Vaccine development process](https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/advice-and-prevention/vaccination/vaccine-development-process)
- [Demystifying beliefs regarding the risks of vaccination](https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/advice-and-prevention/vaccination/demystifying-beliefs-regarding-vaccination)

## Vaccine-preventable diseases

Official source: https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/advice-and-prevention/vaccination

You can avoid many infectious diseases by getting vaccinated. These diseases can have serious consequences and sometimes even cause death. Learn about the risks and symptoms associated with these diseases and get vaccinated to be protected against them. The following diseases are vaccine-preventable:

- [Chickenpox](https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/health-issues/a-z/chickenpox)
- [Cholera](https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/health-issues/a-z/cholera)
- [COVID‑19](https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/health-issues/a-z/2019-coronavirus)
- [Diphtheria](https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/health-issues/a-z/diphtheria)
- [ETEC diarrhea](https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/health-issues/a-z/etec-diarrhea)
- [Flu](https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/health-issues/flu-cold-and-gastroenteritis/flu-influenza)
- [Hepatitis A and hepatitis B](https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/health-issues/stbbis/hepatitis-a-b-and-c)
- [Human papillomavirus (HPV) infections](https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/health-issues/stbbis/human-papillomavirus-hpv)
- [Japanese encephalitis](https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/health-issues/a-z/japanese-encephalitis)
- [Measles](https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/health-issues/a-z/measles)
- [Meningococcal infections](https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/health-issues/a-z/meningococcus)
- [Mpox (monkey pox)](https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/health-issues/a-z/monkeypox)
- [Mumps](https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/health-issues/a-z/mumps)
- [Pneumococcal infections](https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/health-issues/a-z/pneumococcal-infections)
- [Poliomyelitis](https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/health-issues/a-z/poliomyelitis)
- [Respiratory syncytial virus (RSV) infections](https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/health-issues/a-z/respiratory-syncytial-virus-rsv-infections)
- [Rotavirus gastroenteritis](https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/health-issues/flu-cold-and-gastroenteritis/gastroenteritis)
- [Rubella](https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/health-issues/a-z/rubella)
- [Serious _Haemophilus influenzae_ type b (Hib) infections](https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/health-issues/a-z/serious-haemophilus-influenzae-type-b-hib-infections)
- [Shingles](https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/health-issues/a-z/shingles)
- [Tetanus](https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/health-issues/a-z/tetanus)
- [Typhoid](https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/health-issues/a-z/typhoid)
- [Whooping cough](https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/health-issues/a-z/whooping-cough)
- [Yellow fever](https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/health-issues/a-z/yellow-fever)

## Programs and services

Official source: https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/advice-and-prevention/vaccination

The Québec Immunization Program offers certain vaccines for free. To learn more, consult the page [The Québec Immunization Program](https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/advice-and-prevention/vaccination/quebec-immunisation-program).

There are various programs in Québec to facilitate the public’s access to vaccination services. To learn more, consult the following pages:

- [Human Papillomavirus (HPV) Vaccination Program](https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/advice-and-prevention/vaccination/human-papillomavirus-hpv-vaccination-program)
- [Motivational Interviewing in the Maternity Ward for the Immunization of Children (EMMIE) Program](https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/advice-and-prevention/vaccination/motivational-interviewing-in-maternity-for-the-immunization)
- [Québec Vaccination Registry](https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/your-health-information/quebec-vaccination-registry)

Before travelling, make sure you get all the necessary vaccines for the places you are going. The most frequently administered vaccines for travellers are the following:

- [Cholera and ETEC diarrhea vaccine](https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/advice-and-prevention/vaccination/cholera-and-etec-diarrhea-vaccine)

- [Oral typhoid vaccine](https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/advice-and-prevention/vaccination/oral-typhoid-vaccine)
