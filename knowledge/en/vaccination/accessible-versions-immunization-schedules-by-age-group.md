# Accessible versions of the immunization schedules by age group

## Immunization schedule for infants

Official source: https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/advice-and-prevention/vaccination/quebec-immunisation-program/accessible-versions-immunization-schedules-by-age-group

This is the immunization schedule for infants:

- [Preventive treatment against respiratory syncytial virus (RSV)](https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/advice-and-prevention/vaccination/immunization-against-respiratory-syncytial-rsv-infections) is given at birth during the season when the virus is circulating. This preventive treatment provides babies with antibodies already manufactured to protect them throughout the season
- The [diphtheria, tetanus, pertussis (whooping cough), hepatitis B, poliomyelitis and Hib infection vaccine (DTaP-HB-IPV-Hib)](https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/advice-and-prevention/vaccination/combined-dtap-hb-ipv-hib-and-dtap-ipv-hib-vaccines) is given at 2 months and 4 months
- The [pneumococcal vaccine](https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/advice-and-prevention/vaccination/pneumococcal-vaccination-program) is given at 2 months, 4 months and 12 months
- The [rotavirus vaccine](https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/advice-and-prevention/vaccination/rotavirus-vaccine) is given at 2 months and 4 months
- The [diphtheria, pertussis (whooping cough), tetanus, poliomyelitis and Hib infection vaccine (DTaP-IPV-Hib)](https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/advice-and-prevention/vaccination/combined-dtap-hb-ipv-hib-and-dtap-ipv-hib-vaccines) is given at 2 months and 4 months
- The [meningococcal vaccine](https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/advice-and-prevention/vaccination/meningococcal-vaccines) is given at 18 months
- The [measles, mumps, rubella and chickenpox (varicella) vaccine](https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/advice-and-prevention/vaccination/measle-mumps-rubella-chicken-pox) is given at 12 months and 18 months
- The [hepatitis A and hepatitis B vaccine](https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/advice-and-prevention/vaccination/hepatitis-a-and-b-vaccine) is given at 18 months

## Immunization schedule for school-age children

Official source: https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/advice-and-prevention/vaccination/quebec-immunisation-program/accessible-versions-immunization-schedules-by-age-group

This is the immunization schedule for school-age children:

- The [tetanus, diphtheria, pertussis (whooping cough) and poliomyelitis vaccine (Tdap-IPV)](https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/advice-and-prevention/vaccination/diphtheria-pertussis-tetanus-polio-vaccine-4-years-and-older) is given between 4 and 6 years of age
- The [hepatitis A vaccine](https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/advice-and-prevention/vaccination/hepatitis-a-vaccine) is given in Grade 4 of primary school
- The [human papillomavirus (HPV) vaccine](https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/advice-and-prevention/vaccination/human-papillomavirus-hpv-vaccine) is given in Grade 4 of primary school
- The [tetanus and diphtheria vaccine](https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/advice-and-prevention/vaccination/combined-tetanus-and-diphtheria-vaccine-td) is given in Secondary 3 of high school
- The [meningococcal vaccine](https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/advice-and-prevention/vaccination/meningococcal-vaccines) is given in Secondary 3 of high school
- The [hepatitis B vaccine](https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/advice-and-prevention/vaccination/hepatitis-b-vaccine) is given in Secondary 3 of high school

## Immunization schedule for adults

Official source: https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/advice-and-prevention/vaccination/quebec-immunisation-program/accessible-versions-immunization-schedules-by-age-group

This is the immunization schedule for adults:

- The [whooping cough vaccine](https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/health-issues/a-z/whooping-cough#c6954) is recommended for pregnant women of all ages; one dose during each pregnancy, ideally between the 26th and the 32nd week. The vaccine can be given at other times during pregnancy if it is not possible between the 26th and the 32nd week. Consult a doctor or a nurse for more details
- A dose of the [tetanus and diphteria vaccine (Td)](https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/advice-and-prevention/vaccination/combined-tetanus-and-diphtheria-vaccine-td) is recommended at 50 years of age
- The [pneumococcus vaccine](https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/advice-and-prevention/vaccination/pneumococcal-vaccination-program) is recommended for people age 65 years and older
- The [flu vaccine](https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/advice-and-prevention/vaccination/flu-vaccine) (offered every year in fall and winter) is recommended every year from age 65
- The [shingles vaccine](https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/advice-and-prevention/vaccination/shingles-vaccination-program) is recommended for people aged 71 years and older
