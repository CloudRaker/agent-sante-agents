## Vaccination, the best protection

Official source: https://publications.msss.gouv.qc.ca/msss/fichiers/fiches_vaccins/20-278-17A.pdf

By having your child vaccinated, you are protecting him or her against measles, mumps, rubella, varicella and their complications.

|**Diseases**|**Signs and symptoms**|**Possible complications**|
|---|---|---|
|**Measles**|Skin rash<br>Coughing<br>Fever<br>Conjunctivitis (red eyes)<br>General feeling of illness|Ear infection (7 to 9% of cases)<br>Pneumonia (1 to 6% of cases)<br>Convulsions<br>Permanent brain damage (1 case in 1,000 to 2,000)<br>Death (1 case in 3,000)|
|**Mumps**|Fever<br>Headache<br>Swollen glands near the jaw|Meningitis (1 to 10% of cases)<br>Deafness (becoming deaf)<br>Testicular infection<br>Ovarian infection|
|**Rubella**|Skin rash<br>Swollen lymph nodes<br>Arthritis, especially among women|Encephalitis (brain infection) (1 case in 6,000)<br>Miscarriage in pregnant women<br>Malformations in baby whose mother contracted rubella<br>during pregnancy|
|**Varicella**|Fever<br>Varicella blisters (tiny blisters that form a crust<br>and dry up)<br>Itching|Ear infection<br>Pneumonia<br>Infected varicella blister (e.g. impetigo)<br>Serious infection (e.g. flesh eating bacteria)<br>Encephalitis (brain infection)<br>Malformations in baby whose mother contracted<br>varicella during pregnancy<br>Shingles (15 to 30% of cases)<br>Death|

## **Information on the** MMR-Var Vaccine

Official source: https://publications.msss.gouv.qc.ca/msss/fichiers/fiches_vaccins/20-278-17A.pdf

Vaccination is the best protection against measles, mumps, rubella, varicella and their complications. Immunization schedule includes administration of 1 dose at the age of 12 and 18 months. Protection against these diseases lasts throughout life.

**Some symptoms may be caused by the vaccine (e.g. redness at the injection site). Other problems may occur by chance and are not related to the vaccine (e.g. cold, gastro, headache).**

**MMR-Var vaccine is safe. Most reactions are harmless and do not last long.**

We recommend that you stay at the clinic for at least 15 minutes after vaccination because allergic reactions may occur. If an allergic reaction occurs, the symptoms will appear a few minutes after the vaccination. The person giving the vaccine will be able to treat this reaction immediately.
