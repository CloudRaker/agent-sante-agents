Official source: https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/advice-and-prevention/vaccination/vaccination-against-respiratory-infections

## Making an appointment

Official source: https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/advice-and-prevention/vaccination/vaccination-against-respiratory-infections

You can book an appointment on Clic-Santé.

[Make an appointment](https://portal3.clicsante.ca/)

You can also call [1-877-644-4545](tel:+18776444545) on:

- Monday to Friday, 8 a.m. to 8 p.m.
- Saturdays and Sundays from 8 a.m. to 6 p.m.

## Immunization Against Respiratory Syncytial Virus (RSV)

Official source: https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/advice-and-prevention/vaccination/vaccination-against-respiratory-infections

An immunization campaign is underway for people at risk of developing complications from RSV.

Go to the [Respiratory syncytial virus (RSV) immunization](https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/advice-and-prevention/vaccination/immunization-against-respiratory-syncytial-rsv-infections) page to find out more about preventive treatment, the vaccine and the people concerned.

## Administration of several vaccines during the same visit

Official source: https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/advice-and-prevention/vaccination/vaccination-against-respiratory-infections

Depending on your eligibility, you may receive more than one vaccine during the same vaccination session. Getting several vaccines on the same day is a common, safe and widespread practice around the world.
