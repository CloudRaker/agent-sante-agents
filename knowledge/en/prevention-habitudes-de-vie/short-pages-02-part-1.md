## See your dentist regularly (https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/advice-and-prevention/oral-health/preventing-tooth-decay/visit-a-dentist-regularly)

Official source: https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/advice-and-prevention/oral-health/preventing-tooth-decay/visit-a-dentist-regularly

You should see a dentist at least once a year to have your teeth cleaned and your mouth examined. The dentist will decide how often you should have check-ups based on your oral health. An annual check-up may be enough for some people, but others will have to have their teeth checked more often.

If you take medication, tell your dentist, especially if it makes your mouth dry.

You can find a dentist on the [Ordre des dentistes du Québec](https://www.odq.qc.ca/en/trouver-un-dentiste/) website (in French only).

### Take your children to the dentist

Young children should see a dentist for the first time when they are **around 1 year old**. During this first visit, the dentist will:

- examine the child’s mouth before a possible dental problem develops;
- advise parents on how to clean and take care of their child’s mouth properly.

After the first visit, have your child’s teeth checked by a dentist at least once a year, or as recommended. This will allow the dentist to:

- ensure your child’s teeth are cleaned professionally so that his mouth stays clean and healthy;
- promptly identify dental problems and prevent cavities from developing.

If necessary, the dentist can also recommend ways to lower the risk of your child getting cavities. These include:

- **fluoride supplements**
    A dentist can prescribe fluoride supplements for children at risk of tooth decay and who live in areas where the water does not contain enough fluoride.
- **fluoride treatment**
    A dentist or dental hygienist can apply fluoride varnish on your child’s teeth. Fluoride strengthens tooth enamel and makes teeth more resistant to acid, which attacks teeth and causes tooth decay.
- **application of dental sealants**
    A dentist or dental hygienist can apply a dental sealant on some permanent teeth. A dental sealant is a liquid substance that coats the grooves of teeth and hardens. The surface of the teeth becomes smoother and easier to clean. This is very effective in preventing tooth decay.

### Services covered by the Régie de l’assurance maladie du Québec

Some dental services, including annual check-ups, are free for children under 10 years of age. These services are offered at dental clinics or in some hospitals upon presentation of your child’s health insurance card. Go to the [Régie de l’assurance maladie du Québec](https://www.ramq.gouv.qc.ca/en/citizens/health-insurance/dental-services) website for more details.

## Breast changes (https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/advice-and-prevention/screening-and-carrier-testing-offer/breast-cancer-screening/breast-changes)

Official source: https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/advice-and-prevention/screening-and-carrier-testing-offer/breast-cancer-screening/breast-changes

See a doctor or a health-care professional right away if you notice changes in your breasts as described in the table. You should consult your doctor **even if you have recently had a mammogram and the result was normal.**

|
Breast changes

 |

Nipple changes

- A lump in the breast
- The skin of a breast has an inward fold (retraction)

- One breast is red, and the redness covers at least a third of it

- A nipple that points inward into the breast

## About breast cancer screening (https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/advice-and-prevention/screening-and-carrier-testing-offer/breast-cancer-screening/description)

Official source: https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/advice-and-prevention/screening-and-carrier-testing-offer/breast-cancer-screening/description

Breast cancer screening is checking for breast cancer in women who look healthy before signs and symptoms of the disease appear. Breast cancer cannot yet be prevented, but screening for it helps find the disease when it is still in the early stages. This increases the chances of healing.

Breast cancer is:

- the most common cancer among women in Québec
- the second leading cause of death from cancer among women in Québec

In Québec, the only breast cancer screening method recommended is a mammogram.

## Other examinations for breast cancer screening (not recommended) (https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/advice-and-prevention/screening-and-carrier-testing-offer/breast-cancer-screening/other-screening-examinations-not-recommended)

Official source: https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/advice-and-prevention/screening-and-carrier-testing-offer/breast-cancer-screening/other-screening-examinations-not-recommended

A mammography is the only recommended procedure for breast cancer screening in Québec. There are other methods to detect an abnormality, including:

- Breast self-examination
- Clinical breast examination

**Breast self-examination** is a screening method in which women use a specific technique to observe and touch their breasts on a regular basis. The goal of breast self-examination is to discover changes: a sign or lump that could indicate the presence of breast cancer.

Women were once taught breast self-examination to screen for breast cancer. However, studies showed that this method did not reduce breast cancer deaths and that it led to other unnecessary tests. Women who perform breast self-examinations do not reap the benefits but instead suffer inconveniences. As a result, breast self-examination is not recommended as an effective screening method. However, you must remain attentive and inform your doctor of any [changes in your breasts](https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/advice-and-prevention/screening-and-carrier-testing-offer/breast-cancer-screening/breast-changes).

A **clinical breast examination** is performed by a healthcare professional. The exam consists of observing and touching the women’s breasts and armpits to check for abnormalities. The exam can be used to verify certain symptoms or be done for other medical reasons.

However, no study has shown that clinical breast examination is a screening method that could reduce breast cancer mortality. This exam is therefore not recommended as a screening method but may be used as a follow-up to a mammogram.

## About the Québec Breast Cancer Screening Program (https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/advice-and-prevention/screening-and-carrier-testing-offer/quebec-breast-cancer-screening-program/description)

Official source: https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/advice-and-prevention/screening-and-carrier-testing-offer/quebec-breast-cancer-screening-program/description

The Ministère de la Santé et des Services sociaux launched the Québec Breast Cancer Screening Program (PQDCS) in May 1998.

The PQDCS invites women aged 50 to 74 to have a mammogram every 2 years. A mammogram is the only screening exam that can reduce the number of deaths from breast cancer.

The PQDCS’s objective is to reduce breast cancer deaths in women between the ages of 50 and 74 by 25 %. Results obtained since the beginning of the PQDCS are positive and justify continuing the Program.

### Voluntary participation

Taking part in the breast cancer screening program is voluntary. It is important to be well informed on all aspects of the screening before deciding whether to have a mammogram or not. To help you make your decision, read [Advantages, disadvantages and limitations of screening mammography](https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/advice-and-prevention/screening-and-carrier-testing-offer/breast-cancer-screening/advantages-disadvantges-and-limitations-of-mammography).

## Eligibility (https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/advice-and-prevention/screening-and-carrier-testing-offer/quebec-breast-cancer-screening-program/eligibility)

Official source: https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/advice-and-prevention/screening-and-carrier-testing-offer/quebec-breast-cancer-screening-program/eligibility

The PQDCS is specifically aimed at women who are:

- Between the ages of 50 and 74
- Covered by Québec’s public health insurance plan

Women who have already had breast cancer are not eligible for the PQDCS. These women must have personalized medical care that is adapted to their situation.

Women who are under 50 and over 74 must talk to their doctors about the advantages and disadvantages of screening mammography. Depending on their personal risk for breast cancer, the doctor can prescribe a mammogram or another appropriate medical exam.

## Receipt of the test kit and transmission of the results (https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/health-issues/a-z/recessive-hereditary-diseases/offer-of-carrier-screening-tests-for-four-recessive-genetic-disorders-in-people-from-the-regions-of-saguenay-lac-saint-jean-charlevoix-and-haute-cote-nord/receipt-test-kit-transmission-results)

Official source: https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/health-issues/a-z/recessive-hereditary-diseases/offer-of-carrier-screening-tests-for-four-recessive-genetic-disorders-in-people-from-the-regions-of-saguenay-lac-saint-jean-charlevoix-and-haute-cote-nord/receipt-test-kit-transmission-results

When you receive the test kit:

- collect your sample by following the instructions provided **in the kit**;
- read, complete and sign the consent form you received with the test kit;
- return the kit containing your sample and your signed consent form in the reply envelope. The tests are free, but people who do them must cover the cost of mailing their sample to the laboratory.

### Transmission of the results

The laboratory will mail your results to you three to five weeks after receiving your sample.

If you or your partner are carriers of a variant in a gene or if you are both carriers of a variant in the same gene, a health professional will call you.

When carrier screening is done, personal information will be collected about you, including your test results. It will be kept confidentially in your medical record at the Centre intégré universitaire de santé et de services sociaux du Saguenay–Lac-Saint-Jean, in accordance with the legal requirements the health institution must comply with.

The oral cell sample (taken from your mouth) will be kept in the laboratory for five years. It can only be used:

- for research on new genetic tests;
- for laboratory evaluation or quality assurance purposes.

If you want your sample to be kept for more than 5 years, you can indicate this on your consent form.

The laboratory of the Centre intégré universitaire de santé et de services sociaux (CIUSSS) du Saguenay–Lac-Saint-Jean is responsible for offering carrier screening.

## Step 3: Participate in carrier screening (https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/health-issues/a-z/recessive-hereditary-diseases/offer-of-carrier-screening-tests-for-four-recessive-genetic-disorders-in-people-from-the-regions-of-saguenay-lac-saint-jean-charlevoix-and-haute-cote-nord/step-3-participate-in-carrier-screening)

Official source: https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/health-issues/a-z/recessive-hereditary-diseases/offer-of-carrier-screening-tests-for-four-recessive-genetic-disorders-in-people-from-the-regions-of-saguenay-lac-saint-jean-charlevoix-and-haute-cote-nord/step-3-participate-in-carrier-screening

To participate in carrier screening, you must complete two online forms:

- complete the online comprehension questionnaire first;
- then complete the form to request carrier screening.

**Important: you must fill out one form completely in order to access the next one.**

When you have finished filling out the online form, click on "Submit your request".

In three to five business days, you will receive by mail:

- a test kit;
- a consent form;
- a reply envelope.

> ## 12 to 16 weeks pregnant
>
> If you are **12 to 16 weeks pregnant inclusively** or if your **partner is 12 to 16 weeks pregnant inclusively**, your request will have to be dealt with quickly.
>
> 1.  Indicate that you are pregnant or that your partner is pregnant by checking the appropriate box on your online carrier screening request form. **Make sure your partner has also submitted a request**.
> 2.  Call [**1 888 541-1007**](tel:+18885411007) **(reception of the CIUSSS du Saguenay–Lac-Saint-Jean laboratory)**. This way, your request will be dealt with more quickly.
> 3.  The laboratory will mail your results to you one to two weeks after receiving your sample.
