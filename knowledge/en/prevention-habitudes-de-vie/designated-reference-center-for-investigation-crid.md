# Designated Reference Centre for Investigation (CRID)

Official source: https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/advice-and-prevention/screening-and-carrier-testing-offer/quebec-breast-cancer-screening-program/regional-resources/designated-reference-center-for-investigation-crid

Designated Referral Centres (CRID) are hospital centres that provide free [additional examinations](https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/advice-and-prevention/screening-and-carrier-testing-offer/breast-cancer-screening/additional-examinations) recommended under the PQDCS.

Just like Designated Screening Centres, CRID must meet PQDCS quality standards. CRID must all be accredited by the [Canadian Association of Radiologists](https://car.ca/)’ Mammography Accreditation Program and have certification in mammography from the [Laboratoire de santé publique du Québec](https://www.inspq.qc.ca/lspq/accueil) (in French only).

To find contact information for CRID in your region, consult the following section.

To find out about wait times at a CRID to receive an appointment for certain additional examinations under the Québec Breast Cancer Screening Program, consult the [Wait times for access to mammography and other examinations](https://www.inspq.qc.ca/sites/default/files/pqdcs-sondages/delais.html) page on the website of the Institut national de santé publique du Québec (in French only).
