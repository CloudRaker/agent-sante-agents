# Assisted-reproduction centres

Official source: https://www.quebec.ca/en/family-and-support-for-individuals/pregnancy-parenthood/assisted-reproduction/centres

> During the first year of the program, there were some delays in obtaining in vitro fertilization (IVF) treatments. Consequently, some women lost their eligibility for services insured by the Régie de l’assurance maladie du Québec (RAMQ) due to their age.
>
> Therefore, a [temporary reimbursement program](https://www.quebec.ca/en/family-and-support-for-individuals/pregnancy-parenthood/assisted-reproduction/medically-assisted-reproduction-program) has been established to reimburse the expenses incurred by the affected women and their families. It is **effective since June 1, 2022**.

In Québec, clinics require a license to provide assisted reproduction services. They may be located in public hospitals or in private practices.

## Assisted-reproduction centres in public institutions

Official source: https://www.quebec.ca/en/family-and-support-for-individuals/pregnancy-parenthood/assisted-reproduction/centres

Some hospitals and public establishments provide fertility exams and some medically assisted reproduction (MAR) treatments. These hospital-based assisted reproduction centres provide insured services as well as services at patient’s expense.

Treatments available at these centres may vary. They offer the following services:

- Stimulation or induction of ovulation
- Artificial insemination, including monitoring ovarian follicles with ultrasound, with sperm from a spouse, sperm bank or directed donation (known donor)
- Some medical acts necessary for in vitro fertilization as well as patient support after embryo transfer.

## Assisted-reproduction centres offering insured services of IVF

Official source: https://www.quebec.ca/en/family-and-support-for-individuals/pregnancy-parenthood/assisted-reproduction/centres

If in vitro fertilization (IVF) is the treatment indicated for your circumstances, you will be referred to an assisted-reproduction centre that does it.

It is possible that your regional centre could, before transferring you, handle the initial ovarian stimulation and start ultrasound monitoring of ovarian follicles, which will continue until the [egg retrieval](https://www.quebec.ca/en/family-and-support-for-individuals/pregnancy-parenthood/assisted-reproduction/how-receive#c118306).

Assisted-Reproduction Centres offering insured IVF are located in university hospitals and private clinics.
