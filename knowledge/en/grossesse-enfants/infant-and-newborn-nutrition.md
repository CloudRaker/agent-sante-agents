# Infant and newborn nutrition

Official source: https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/nutrition/healthy-eating-habits/infant-and-newborn-nutrition

The birth of a baby brings up a lot of questions, especially with regard to nutrition.

The Institut national de santé publique du Québec’s [From Tiny Tot to Toddler: A Practical Guide for Parents from Pregnancy to Age Two](https://www.inspq.qc.ca/en/tiny-tot) offers information on nutrition for babies. Among other issues, it answers questions about breastfeeding, how to introduce new foods, and food allergies.

## Help and resources

Official source: https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/nutrition/healthy-eating-habits/infant-and-newborn-nutrition

#### Resources for help and support

- [Info-Santé 811](https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/finding-a-resource/info-sante-811) is a telephone consultation service available 24 hours a day, 365 days a year, everywhere in Québec

To obtain information tailored to your needs, contact one of the following resources:

- Your [child's doctor or specialized nurse](http://sante.gouv.qc.ca/en/repertoire-ressources/recherche/?typeRech=type&cht[]=2)
- Your [CLSC](https://sante.gouv.qc.ca/en/repertoire-ressources/clsc/)

#### Breastfeeding support

- The [Ligue La Leche](https://allaitement.ca/) is established in most regions of Québec (website in French only). Certified volunteers offer help and support to breastfeeding women. You can receive support:
    - By phone: 1-866-ALLAITER ([1-866-255-2483](tel:+18662552483))
    - By email: [information@allaitement.ca](mailto:information@allaitement.ca)
- The [Nourri-Source Federation](http://www.nourri-source.org/) (support group, website in French only) is active in six regions, including Estrie, Lanaudière, Laurentides, Laval, Montérégie, Montreal and Saguenay–Lac-Saint-Jean. You can receive support:
    - By phone: [1-866-948-5160](tel:+18669485160)
    - By email: [info@nourri-source.org](mailto:info@nourri-source.org)
- A lactation consultant can also help you. To find a consultant in your region, visit the website of the [International Board Certified Lactation Consultants](https://www.aqclactation.ca/en/accueil) (IBCLC). You can also reach them:
    - By phone: [514-990-0262](tel:+15149900262)
    - By email: [info@ibclc.qc.ca](mailto:info@ibclc.qc.ca)
