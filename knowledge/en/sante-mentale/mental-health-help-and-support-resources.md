# Mental health help and support resources

Official source: https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/mental-health/finding-help-and-support-for-mental-health/finding-mental-health-help-and-support-resources/mental-health-help-and-support-resources

Find out about available mental health help and support resources, as needed.

> Call [**Info-Social 811**](tel:811) to get help or information regarding your mental health or the mental health of a family member or friend.
>
> Call the [**911 emergency service**](tel:911), if you fear for your safety or the safety of a family member or friend, or to obtain immediate assistance.
>
> Call the **suicide prevention and help line:** [**1‑866‑APPELLE (277‑3553)**](tel:+18662773553), if you are thinking about suicide or if you are worried about a loved one (to send text messages (SMS): 535353, to chat: [suicide.ca](https://suicide.ca/en/chatting-online-with-a-counsellor)).
>
> Find a [crisis centre](https://resicq.ca/resicq-en/) (in French only) near you to obtain free specialized crisis intervention services for you or a loved one.

To obtain care or services or to find a professional with whom you feel comfortable, contact one of the following resources:

- [Info-Social 811](https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/finding-a-resource/info-social-811)
- A [doctor](https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/finding-a-resource/family-doctor-schedule-an-appointment) or the [Primary Care Access Point](https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/finding-a-resource/primary-care-access-point)
- A [local community services centres (CLSC)](https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/health-system-and-services/service-organization/clsc)
- A mental health professional in a private practice (psychologist, psychotherapist, social worker, etc.)

Organizations are also available in your community. Consult the list below or use the [Mental Health Resource Directory](https://sante.gouv.qc.ca/en/repertoire-ressources/sante-mentale/) to find a resource near you.

## Suicide prevention

Official source: https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/mental-health/finding-help-and-support-for-mental-health/finding-mental-health-help-and-support-resources/mental-health-help-and-support-resources

Talk anytime, anywhere in Québec, with a professional who is able to speak openly about suicide:

- By telephone: suicide prevention and help line: 1-866-APPELLE or [1-866-277-3553](tel:+18662773553)
- By text (SMS): 535353
- By chat: [suicide.ca](https://suicide.ca/)

Also see the page [Recognizing signs of distress and preventing suicide](https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/mental-health/learn-about-mental-health-and-mental-illnesses/recognizing-signs-of-distress-and-preventing-suicide).
