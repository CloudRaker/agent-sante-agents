## Do you need to talk to someone right away?

Official source: https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/mental-health

Caseworkers are available to help you 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. Many organizations offer free and confidential support services and professional consultations. Contact them:

- [Info-Social 811](tel:811) (811, option 2) – To quickly reach a professional in psychosocial intervention
- [Local community services centre (CLSC)](https://sante.gouv.qc.ca/en/repertoire-ressources/clsc/) – To receive support and help if you have symptoms of mental disorder or a social problem
- [1-866-APPELLE](tel:+18662773553) (1-866-277-3553) - text at [535353](sms:535353) - [live chat](https://suicide.ca/en/chatting-online-with-a-counsellor) – To find support if you or a loved one is considering suicide
- [911](tel:911) – In case of immediate danger to yourself or a loved one

Other [help lines and telephone services](https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/mental-health/finding-help-and-support-for-mental-health/finding-mental-health-help-and-support-resources/mental-health-help-and-support-resources#c120026) are also available, consult their information.
