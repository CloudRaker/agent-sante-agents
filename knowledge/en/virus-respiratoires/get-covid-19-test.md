# Test for flu-like symptoms or symptoms similar to COVID-19

Official source: https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/advice-and-prevention/screening-and-carrier-testing-offer/testing-flu-like-symptoms/get-covid-19-test

> The systematic offer of rapid tests for COVID-19 screening has been discontinued. Rapid tests may no longer be available at certain local points of service.
>
> People eligible for local points of service testing for influenza-like symptoms or COVID-19 can make an [appointment](https://portal3.clicsante.ca/services/depistage-de-la-covid-19).

## When to get tested

Official source: https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/advice-and-prevention/screening-and-carrier-testing-offer/testing-flu-like-symptoms/get-covid-19-test

If you have flu-like [symptoms similar to those of COVID-19](https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/health-issues/a-z/2019-coronavirus/symptoms-transmission-treatment), it is generally not necessary to get tested.

The most important thing is to follow the [steps for limiting the spread of respiratory infectious diseases](https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/advice-and-prevention/accidents-injuries-and-diseases-prevention/steps-limiting-spread-respiratory-infectious-diseases-1).

## Who can get tested at a local point of service

Official source: https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/advice-and-prevention/screening-and-carrier-testing-offer/testing-flu-like-symptoms/get-covid-19-test

You can get tested at a local point of service if:

- you are a healthcare and social services workers;
- you have COVID-19 symptoms and you may benefit from a COVID-19 treatment such as [PaxlovidTM](https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/health-issues/a-z/2019-coronavirus/symptoms-transmission-treatment/oral-treatment-against-covid-19-paxlovidmc).

[Book your appointment to get tested](https://portal3.clicsante.ca/)

If you are concerned, you can also make an appointment at a local point of service by calling [1-877-644-4545](tel:18776444545).

## Contraindications to getting tested for COVID-19

Official source: https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/advice-and-prevention/screening-and-carrier-testing-offer/testing-flu-like-symptoms/get-covid-19-test

There are no contraindications. However, some situations require a medical assessment before getting tested:

- you have a nasal obstruction other than nasal congestion;
- you are having a nosebleed now;
- you had a nosebleed in the last week;
- you have had surgery:
    - oral surgery in the last week;
    - nose surgery in the last month;
- you are having an episode of wheezing.
