## Diphtérie (https://www.quebec.ca/sante/problemes-de-sante/a-z/diphterie)

Source officielle : https://www.quebec.ca/sante/problemes-de-sante/a-z/diphterie

### Signes et symptômes

- Maux de gorge importants
- Forte fièvre
- Problèmes respiratoires et cardiaques

### Complications possibles

- Paralysie
- Décès (5 à 10 % des cas)

### Vaccins disponibles

- [Vaccin DCaT-HB-VPI-Hib et DCaT-VPI-Hib](https://www.quebec.ca/sante/conseils-et-prevention/vaccination/vaccins-combines-dcat-hb-vpi-hib-ou-dcat-vpi-hib)
- [Vaccin DCaT-VPI – Moins de 4 ans](https://www.quebec.ca/sante/conseils-et-prevention/vaccination/vaccin-diphterie-coqueluche-tetanos-et-poliomyelite-moins-de-4-ans)
- [Vaccin dcaT](https://www.quebec.ca/sante/conseils-et-prevention/vaccination/vaccin-diphterie-coqueluche-et-tetanos)
- [Vaccin dcaT-VPI – 4 ans et plus](https://www.quebec.ca/sante/conseils-et-prevention/vaccination/diphterie-coqueluche-tetanos-poliomyelite-4-ans-et-plus)
- [Vaccin dT](https://www.quebec.ca/sante/conseils-et-prevention/vaccination/vaccin-diphterie-et-tetanos)

Consultez le [calendrier régulier de vaccination](https://www.quebec.ca/sante/conseils-et-prevention/vaccination/programme-quebecois-d-immunisation#c2534).
